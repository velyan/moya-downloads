## You never brief her

Using a typical AI chat means copying the doc, thread or design, opening another tab, explaining what you're working on, and pasting the answer back, every time. Moya starts from what's already on your screen, so the context is there when you need to decide.

## What Moya does

- **Review a doc.** Select a line in a doc you're about to share and ask whether it's right. Moya checks it against your connected folder as well as the doc.
- **Reply from your files.** Type "Moya" in a reply and she drafts the answer in place from your files, such as pricing or security docs. Nothing is sent for you.
- **Get a second opinion.** Ask a Moya team of role-based reviewers (for example an engineer, a product builder and an operations coordinator) what a plan is missing. Each one pushes back, and you make the call.
- **Keep your promises.** Type "I'll get back to you tomorrow" and Moya offers a reminder, then brings you back to the thread when it's time.
- **Save real files.** Ask for a brief, a summary or a draft and she saves a real file to the folder you choose.
- **Works in any text field.** Type "Moya" and an instruction in any app's text field, including web forms, and she answers in place. You decide what gets sent or submitted.

## Privacy

Moya is built to sit beside your work, not to collect it.

- **On your Mac:** local models power proactive support. Your screen history and memories are stored on your Mac.
- **Only when you ask:** relevant context from your current window is sent through your OpenAI account only when you ask.
- **Under your control:** you choose which apps Moya sees and which folders she reads and writes. You can exclude apps and websites, turn Personal Memory off, or delete captured history in Settings.
- **Visible:** Privacy & Activity shows prompts, replies and available context summaries.
- **Drafts, not actions:** Moya prepares advice and drafts for you to review. She doesn't send messages for you.
- **Permission-based:** screen capture requires macOS permission. Folder access and app connections are optional.
